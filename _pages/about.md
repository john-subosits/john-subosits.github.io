Dr. John Subosits is director of the Extreme Performance Intelligent Control (EPIC) department within the Human Interactive Driving (HID) division at Toyota Research Institute (TRI). The EPIC department is developing high-performance physical AI systems that perceive, plan, and adapt as well as the best experts.  Specific projects of the group include control under dynamic, perceptual and state estimation uncertainty, safety filtering, and world modeling.

In the past, he was worked on the demonstration of precise multivehicle control at the limits through autonomous tandem drifting and amplifying the efforts of race engineers and vehicle designers in Toyota’s motorsports and performance vehicle divisions through the creation of driver models for motorsports simulation that served as effective surrogates for professional test drivers.

Dr. Subosits holds a bachelor's degree in mechanical and aerospace engineering from Princeton University and master's and Ph.D. degrees in mechanical engineering from Stanford University. His doctoral work focused on trajectory planning for autonomous vehicles at their performance limits.
